Center third: one full-body archer in lighter teal leather armor holding an elegant recurved bow, quiver visible. Right third: one full-body cavalry soldier mounted on a noble dark horse in jade and antique bronze tack, horse and rider completely inside that right third. Subjects do not overlap other thirds, generous margin at boundaries and top, feet/hooves near bottom. Same scale and realistic detailed hand-painted 3D fantasy game art throughout, warm gold rim lighting and jade armor, dark charcoal-green smoky studio backdrop, not cartoon, not chibi. Generated with the built-in image_gen tool, using the imagegen skill. Existing assets were not overwritten. Text, controls, fog of war and routes are rendered by the game, not baked into the images.
